What this tool can conclude

Directly from the dump

  • Scheduled-open vs revised staffing requirement by interval.
  • Roster patterns, OFF days and shift durations.
  • Logged activity minutes, break/lunch distribution and timing.
  • Employee identity and team mapping across sheets.
  • Missing fields, blank values, duplicate records and likely inconsistencies.

What needs extra inputs

Do not infer without evidence

  • Actual service level, abandonment and adherence when actual fields are empty.
  • Contractual or legal break compliance without the governing policy.
  • Optimal shift changes without shift flexibility, skills and constraints.
  • True labor cost without wage/cost inputs.
  • Forecast accuracy without historical actuals.
