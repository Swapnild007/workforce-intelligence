Projects

End-to-end builds that connect what you learn in Learn, test in WFM Lab, and defend in Decision Lab.

Project 01 · Foundations → WFM

Contact Centre Operating Model

Model queues, skills, channels, service objectives, operating hours, shrinkage and the end-to-end WFM cycle.

Project 02 · Forecast → Staffing

Forecast-to-Staff Engine

Take historical demand through forecast, interval workload, Erlang staffing, shrinkage and capacity requirements.

Project 03 · Scheduling

Schedule & Coverage Optimizer

Turn interval requirements into shift patterns, breaks, activities and coverage measures while exposing constraints and inefficiency.

Project 04 · Intraday

Intraday Command Center

Compare forecast vs actual, AHT, adherence, available staffing and net coverage, then choose controlled interventions.

Project 05 · Analytics

WFM Executive Analytics

Build the KPI layer: SLA, ASA, AHT, abandonment, occupancy, adherence, forecast accuracy, capacity and schedule efficiency.

Project 06 · Capstone

Workforce Intelligence System

Integrate data, forecasting, staffing, scheduling, intraday, analytics, optimization and decision-making into one portfolio-grade system.
